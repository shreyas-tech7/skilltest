# Meeting notes

Turn a raw transcript into notes people will read.

## Steps

1. Find the decisions. A decision is something the group agreed to do or not do.
2. Find the action items. Each one needs an owner and, if said, a due date.
3. Write a summary of at most five lines.
4. List open questions that nobody answered.
5. Fill in [the notes template](assets/template.md).

## Rules

- Quote numbers and dates exactly as said.
- If an owner is unclear, write "owner unclear" instead of guessing.
